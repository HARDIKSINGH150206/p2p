The campus, building by building

Eight buildings, sized by their share of grid electricity over 120 days. Rings mark buildings with a composter; the sun marks rooftop solar.

From 8 to 30+ buildings

  • Alias table. The 37 spellings in the logs map to 8 buildings through a table filled once at onboarding. Unmatched names go to a review queue, never silently dropped.
  • One small model per building. Ridge fits in tens of milliseconds, so it runs centrally for 30 or on an edge box for 300. New buildings start from the day-type profile until they have about two weeks of data.
  • Late-arrival replay. Records order and dedupe by arrival time per gateway. Replay windows are bounded per building (here up to about 12 h).
  • Central aggregation. Only Brief rows (cause, kWh, Rs, evidence) leave the building; raw streams stay local.
